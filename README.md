A short poem about agency: the power to stop and to stay. Built for
make.fun's **"The poem about stopping"** bounty.

One hand hovers over a switch. One press would set the work in motion —
a road never chosen, walked in someone else's name. The hand lifts.
It stays. Nothing moves, and yet everything changes: the power was
there all along, kept whole by not being spent. Stopping is not giving
up agency — it is the moment agency is taken back.
